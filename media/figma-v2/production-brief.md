## Visual direction

Deep evergreen #102E2D, luminous lime #D6EFAE, warm white #F4F7EF. Use 80–104 px headlines, 30–36 px supporting text, generous negative space, layered product panels, and restrained shadows. Keep secondary features grouped so they support the primary story without competing with it. Use 200–350 ms matched transitions, gradual camera crops on real UI footage, and gentle highlights synchronized to the soundtrack. Avoid rapid flashes and simulated sign-language gestures.
